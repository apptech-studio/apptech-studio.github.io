Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I play Miu Live: Idle Streamer?

Tap anywhere on the stream screen to cheer and chat with Miu! Tapping fills the hype meter to trigger Fever Mode for rapid donations. Use earned coins to unlock stylish outfits, upgrade studio gear, and boost your passive streaming income.

Is an internet connection required?

You can enjoy core idle gameplay, tapping, and room upgrades offline. An active internet connection is only required to load optional rewarded ads, synchronize remote game balance updates, or complete Google Play Billing purchases.

How do offline earnings work?

Even when you're away, Miu continues streaming passively! When you reopen the game, a welcome screen will calculate your offline donations based on your current studio upgrade levels.

How do I reset my game progress?

Progress is stored in your device's local application storage. You can reset your progress by clearing app storage via Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Miu Live: Idle Streamer → Storage → Clear Storage) or by uninstalling and reinstalling the game.
