Miu Live: Idle Streamer ("the Game", "we", "us") is an anime-styled idle streamer simulation game for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game handles, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

We believe in privacy by design. The Game is local-first: your game progression, unlocked outfits, room upgrades, and virtual coins are stored directly on your device. We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal details to play.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require user accounts, passwords, or personal identity information.
  • We do not collect or record your camera video, microphone audio, or phone calls.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Game Progress & Settings: Your streamer level, fan count, coins, purchased room items, unlocked outfits, and sound/haptic settings are stored in your device's local application storage. You can reset or remove this data at any time by clearing app storage in device settings or uninstalling the Game.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is rated for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe personal information has inadvertently been collected, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

5. Data Security & Retention

Because your game data is stored in your device's sandboxed storage, it is protected by Android operating system security measures. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local game progress from the device.
